Privacy Policy — Little Krishna's Butter Hunt

Effective date: 2 August 2026  ·  Developer: JPtheDash  ·  Contact: Dijytalwonder@gmail.com

Little Krishna's Butter Hunt does not collect any personal information.

The game has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, and no network features of any kind. It never sends anything anywhere, because it never connects to anything. Everything it saves stays on your own device.

What the app saves on your device

So that you do not lose your place, the game stores a single record on the device itself:

  • which level you have unlocked
  • how many stars you earned on each level
  • whether music and sound effects are switched on

That is the whole of it. It contains no name, no email address, no account, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, and no location.

This record is kept in the app's own private storage, which no other app can read. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can clear it at any time from Android's Settings → Apps → Little Krishna's Butter Hunt → Storage → Clear data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.
  • It does not contain adverts or advertising networks.
  • It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not use cookies or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or any other permission that reaches your personal information.
  • It does not contain any third-party service that could collect data. The game is built on the Phaser game engine and the Capacitor app framework, both of which are bundled into the app and run entirely on your device.

The app's listing declares the INTERNET permission because the app framework it is built with requests it as standard. The game makes no network requests and works fully offline, including in aeroplane mode.

Android's own backup

Android may include the app's saved progress in the device backup it makes to your Google account, if you have device backup switched on. This is a feature of Android, not of this game — the data goes to your own Google Drive, under your control, and the developer has no access to it and never receives it.

You can turn this off in Android's Settings → Google → Backup.

Children's privacy

This game is suitable for children, and it is built so that there is nothing to protect: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Because nothing is collected, nothing is stored off the device, and nothing is shared with anyone, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or under 16, where that is the applicable age — and there is no data for a parent or guardian to request access to or deletion of beyond uninstalling the app.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about this, please use the contact address above.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data held about you. No personal data about you is held, so there is nothing to access, export, or delete. Removing the game's saved progress is done by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a future version added an online feature — this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version is released.
